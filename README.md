Bragging Wall is a private place to record your work wins every day, so they're ready when you need them for a resume, a salary or promotion conversation, or a reminder of what you're capable of on a hard day.

## What you can do

- **Pin a win every day.** Log what you did, its area (Delivery, Quality & Testing, Leadership, Process, Learning, Collaboration, Recognition, Personal), how big it was, and the result or proof.
- **See it on a calendar.** Day, week, month and year views, each with a summary of totals, big wins, top areas and highlights. The year view is a heatmap.
- **Keep notebooks.** Journals, planners and work notebooks with rich-text pages, checklists, and templates for daily plans, meeting notes, QA test sessions, weekly reviews and 1:1 prep. The last two pull in your logged wins automatically.
- **Export PDFs** of a single page, a whole notebook, or your achievement report.
- **Build a report** for a salary or promotion review, your resume, or a cheer-me-up recap, for any date range. Edit the text before you export it.
- **Cheer yourself up** with a random past win.
- **Search** everything with Ctrl/Cmd + K.


### Freedom Walls
Every win you brag is pinned on your Freedom Wall as a sticky note (date, title, feeling, area, stars, a photo if you attached one). Tap a note to read a preview, then open the full win. Make as many walls as you like and pick which one shows on your home page (with one wall, it's shown automatically). Design each wall: draw with pen, marker and highlighter (with eraser and undo), add text, sticky notes, animated stickers, emoji, icons, shapes, your own images and GIFs, and choose a color, gradient, pattern or photo background. Drag wins to rearrange them, tilt them, or put them back in rows. Show all wins, this month, this year or big wins only.

### Win highlights
Make a win stand out with an animated effect: confetti, fireworks, hearts, sparkles, balloons, ladybugs walking around it, a spotlight, a crown, a gold shine, a rainbow border or flames. Pick one from the win page ("Highlight this win"), the win preview on your Freedom Wall, or the wall designer. Effects show on the Freedom Wall, the home carousel and the win page, and stay still if your device is set to reduce motion.

### Share, print and post your Freedom Wall
**Share or print** turns a wall into a branded picture: a 4:5 post, a 9:16 story or the whole wall. Download it, print it, save it as a PDF, use your phone's Share menu, or post it to Facebook, Instagram, X, LinkedIn or Threads (the picture is saved and your caption copied, then the app opens). **Hide win details** keeps work wins private by showing only each win's area and size.

### Saved password and side menu
Save your password on a device for 1, 7, 30 or 90 days or until you forget it, so your wall opens without typing it and backups fill it in. It's encrypted with a key that can't leave that browser. Collapse the side menu to icons with the button next to the logo or Ctrl+\.

### Linked digital planner
In a Planner notebook, start from **Planner index**. Every link opens that page in the notebook, or creates it the first time you tap it:

- **Quarter planner:** three mini months, what the quarter is for, focus by month, dates already fixed, how it went, highlights.
- **Month pages:** at a glance (a line for every day), calendar with important dates, to-dos and next month, overview (focus, priorities, wins, goals, projects, people), budget, review (rate the month) and habit cards. A button row on each jumps between them and to the next or previous month.
- **Week in columns:** the one thing, seven tall columns with energy, weekly anchors, habits and this week's wins.
- **Day, hour by hour:** half-hour schedule from 6 am to 9 pm, mini calendar, one thing, top 3, to-dos, meals, water, sleep, mood and energy, basic needs, grateful for, tomorrow and brain dump. The **+** beside any time adds it to Google Calendar, or downloads an .ics for Apple Calendar, Outlook or Reminders, with an optional reminder.
- Tap any date on a mini calendar or month grid to open that day.

Template library categories: Personal, Self-care, Education, Finance, Budget, Vision, Planning, Household, Work and Travel, plus note paper. Six more page themes: Pastel rainbow, White, Midnight, Pink, Matcha and Boho. Add emoji stickers to any page from the toolbar, and start a notebook cover from one of ten ready-made covers.

### Project manager (Work notebook template)
In a Work notebook, pick **Project manager (automated)** under Project management. The page is the project: enter each task once (owner, phase, status, priority, start, due, progress, cost, repeat) and the page's tabs update by themselves:
- **Dashboard:** progress ring, tasks done, overdue, due this week, days left, status donut, priority and person-in-charge charts, budget vs expenses, overdue / today / coming up lists, a month calendar and a tasks-per-day chart.
- **Tasks:** a spreadsheet-style table with search, filters (status, priority, owner, phase) and sorting.
- **Kanban:** drag cards from Backlog to Done.
- **Gantt:** bars drawn from the dates, grouped by phase, with a today line; drag a bar to move it or its edge to change the due date.
- **Calendar:** tasks on their due dates and repeating tasks on every day they repeat, with weekly totals; tick repeating tasks off per day.
- **Matrix:** open tasks sorted into Do now, Schedule, Delegate and Drop from priority and due date.
The page title is the project name. The project is saved inside the page, so it's encrypted, PIN-locked, backed up, searchable and exported to PDF like any other page. Finishing a task offers to add it to your wall as a win.

### Notebook covers
Design a cover for every notebook: solid colors, gradients, patterns or your own photo or GIF as the background, then add text in four styles, animated stickers, emoji, icons, shapes (washi tape, ribbons, badges, the Bragging Wall logo) and your own images, stickers or GIFs. Drag to move, pull the handles to resize or rotate. Covers show on your bookshelf and in the notebook list. Uploaded pictures are encrypted like everything else.

### Shelves, categories, tags and PIN locks
Organize notebooks on shelves you name and color, and give each notebook a category and tags; filter the bookshelf by any of them. Lock a notebook with a 4 to 12 digit PIN: its pages are encrypted with a key that only your PIN unlocks (AES-256-GCM, PBKDF2 600k), on top of the app's own encryption. Unlocked notebooks lock again when you leave Notebooks or lock the app. Too many wrong tries add a wait. If you have an app password, you can reset a forgotten PIN with it.

### Backup files
**Create backup file** downloads one encrypted `.json` with everything: wins (with stories, feelings and Braddy's comments), attachments, notebooks and pages, notebook covers and their uploaded pictures and GIFs, shelves, categories, tags, custom areas, settings, and notebook PIN locks (locked notebooks stay locked). Every backup is locked with two things: your Bragging Wall password and a 20-character backup code generated for you after the file is created. You enter your password to create the backup and again before downloading, and you need both your password (from the day of the backup) and the code to import. Repeated wrong tries add a wait. Older backups made with your password still import. **Import backup file** merges a backup into the wall without deleting anything. Passwords, recovery codes and PINs are never written into the file in readable form. (Google Drive backup is built but switched off for now.)

### Email your brags
Send a branded PDF brag sheet for one brag, or for every brag in a day, week, month or year. Bragging Wall builds the PDF (logo, header band, sticky-note cards, photos, page footers) and opens Gmail, Outlook or your email app with the subject and a summary already filled in. You attach the PDF there. On phones, **Share PDF** can hand it straight to your mail app. A web page can't send email by itself, so nothing ever leaves your device without you pressing send.

### Braddy, your supporter
Every win gets a comment from **Braddy from Bragging Wall**: an honest, friendly take on the win, notes on your attachments, an animated sticker, a look at your journey (chips and a six-month chart) and one piece of honest advice. In the claude.ai version Braddy is written by Claude and saved with the win (encrypted like everything else). On GitHub Pages, Braddy gives a quick take made on your device.

## Privacy and security

- **Your wall stays in your browser.** There's no server and no account. Entries are stored in this browser on this device, and nothing is sent anywhere.
- **Optional password lock with encryption.** Each entry is encrypted in the browser with AES-256-GCM. The key is protected by your password (PBKDF2-SHA256, 600,000 iterations) and by a one-time recovery code. The password itself is never stored. The wall locks again after a period of inactivity that you choose.
- **Encrypted backups.** Backup files are always encrypted, using either your password or a password you set for the file. Restoring a backup is also how you move your wall to another device or browser.
- **If you forget both your password and your recovery code, nobody can recover your entries.** Keep the recovery code somewhere safe.
- **Spreadsheet (.csv) exports aren't encrypted**, so keep them private.

> Because entries live in one browser, clearing that site's browser data erases them. Download an encrypted backup regularly.

## Design

- **Name and logo:** a wall of bricks with a marigold star on top, for a wall you build one win at a time.
- **Color:** a warm off-white ground (inspired by Pantone's 2026 Cloud Dancer), deep teal for actions and progress, and marigold kept for brag-worthy wins. Light and dark themes are both fully designed.
- **Type:** Fraunces, an expressive variable serif, for headings; Instrument Sans for reading; Caveat for the handwritten notes.
- **Freedom wall:** the background is a Filipino-style freedom wall of sticky notes, taped index cards, pushpins and doodles with encouraging messages ("Kaya mo 'yan!", "Laban lang!", "Proud of you!"). Your three newest wins are pinned as notes on the home screen.
- **Layout:** a bento-style wall of solid cards over the freedom wall, with one localized glow per screen instead of full-page gradients.
- **Motion:** used only to guide the eye (entrances, progress, saving). It's turned off automatically when your device asks for reduced motion.
- **Accessibility:** keyboard-friendly controls, visible focus states, and text contrast designed for both themes.

## Run it yourself

It's a single file. Open `index.html` in a modern browser, or serve the folder with any static host. On GitHub Pages: **Settings → Pages → Deploy from a branch → `main` / root**.

PDFs are generated in the browser with [jsPDF](https://github.com/parallax/jsPDF). Fonts come from Google Fonts.
